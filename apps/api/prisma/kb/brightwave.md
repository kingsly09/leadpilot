# Brightwave Digital — Company overview

Brightwave Digital is a digital agency that helps small and medium businesses get more customers online. We work with clients worldwide, fully remote, and reply to enquiries within one business day. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm (WAT, UTC+1).

# Services and pricing

Website design and development: modern, mobile-friendly websites built on Webflow, WordPress or Next.js. Starter websites (up to 5 pages) start at $1,200. Business websites (up to 15 pages, blog, CMS) start at $2,500. E-commerce stores start at $3,500. Typical delivery is 3–6 weeks.

Social media ads management: we plan, create and manage Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns. Management starts at $500 per month plus ad spend, with a recommended minimum ad spend of $300 per month. Includes monthly reporting.

SEO (search engine optimisation): a one-off SEO audit costs $400 and is delivered in 7 days. Ongoing SEO packages start at $600 per month with a 3-month minimum.

AI chatbots and automation: custom AI chatbots for websites, Facebook Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp that answer questions and capture leads, plus workflow automation with n8n. Projects start at $1,500 and usually take 2–4 weeks.

Branding: logo and brand identity packages start at $600.

# Process

1. Free 30-minute discovery call to understand your goals.
2. Written proposal with fixed price and timeline within 2 business days.
3. 50% deposit to start, 50% on completion. Monthly services are billed at the start of each month.
4. Weekly progress updates and a dedicated project manager.

# FAQ

Do you offer discounts? Pricing is fixed per package; any custom pricing is discussed by our team during the discovery call.

Can I see examples of your work? Yes — our team will share relevant case studies after the discovery call.

Do you offer support after launch? Every website includes 30 days of free support. Ongoing maintenance plans start at $99 per month.

How do I book a call? Share your email and a convenient time, and a team member will send a calendar invite.

What payment methods do you accept? Bank transfer, card payments and PayPal.
